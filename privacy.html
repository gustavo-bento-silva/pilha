Pilha: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Pilha: RPN Financial Calc ("the app"), published by Gustavo Bento ("we"), handles your data.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Numbers you type, your tape and your memories stay on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • No ads, no advertising ID and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are optional and off until you confirm in the app (Google Firebase).
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

  • Calculator stack, registers, tape, settings and purchase status are saved locally.
  • This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. The tape export (text or CSV) only happens when you choose to share it.

Analytics

    We use Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics), only after you confirm in the app. Examples of events: onboarding steps, which function families are used (for example TVM or NPV), error codes shown, purchase screen views and purchases, plus crash reports. We never send the values you type. We also receive basic device and app information such as OS version, app version, language and approximate country.

  • Data is linked to a random installation identifier. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking.

    • Opting out: turn off Settings → Usage statistics and crashes at any time. Turning it off stops collection.

    Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

  • Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. The app only receives confirmation of what you own.
  • You can restore purchases in Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, the simplest way is to turn statistics off in Settings, and you can email us to request deletion. You can also contact us.

Not financial advice

Results are for study and checking your own calculations. They are not financial advice or a credit offer.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
