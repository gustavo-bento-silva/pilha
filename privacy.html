Pilha: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Pilha: RPN Financial Calc ("the app"), published by Gustavo Bento ("we"), handles your data.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Numbers you type, your tape and your memories stay on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • Android (free version): the app shows a banner ad. Ads use your device advertising ID. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent first.
  • iOS: no ads, no advertising ID, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We collect pseudonymous usage analytics (linked only to a random installation ID) to improve the app.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

  • Calculator stack, registers, tape, settings and purchase status are saved locally.
  • This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. The tape export (text or CSV) only happens when you choose to share it.

Analytics

  • We use Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies). Examples of events: onboarding steps, which function families are used (for example TVM or NPV), error codes shown, purchase screen views and purchases. We never send the values you type. We also receive basic device and app information such as OS version, app version, country and diagnostics.
  • Data is linked to a random installation identifier. It is not linked to your name or email and is not used for tracking.
  • Opting out: Settings → Privacy lets you turn statistics off (iOS) or reopen the consent form (Android). Turning it off stops collection and sends a deletion request to Unity.
  • Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Ads (Android free version only)

  • We use Unity LevelPlay to mediate ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. Ad networks may process your advertising ID, IP address (approximate location) and device information to show and measure ads.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, no ad request is made before you answer the consent form. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy.
  • Buying Pilha Full or Remove ads disables the ad SDK in later sessions.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

  • Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. The app only receives confirmation of what you own.
  • You can restore purchases in Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, the simplest way is to turn statistics off in Settings, which requests deletion. You can also contact us.

Not financial advice

Results are for study and checking your own calculations. They are not financial advice or a credit offer.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
